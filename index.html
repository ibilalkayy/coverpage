FAQ

Common questions

How do I start a deal right now?
Scroll down to the "Protected Deals" section on this page. Fill in the product details, amount, and seller's WhatsApp number in the unified deal form, then click "Send Transaction & Deal Details to Cover". No external forms or redirects needed.
How do I pay for my deal?
Scan the QR code in the deal form and transfer the total amount (product + delivery + Cover fee) into Cover's secure hold. The form calculates the exact amount for you automatically.
How do I confirm my payment was received?
After transferring the money, enter your Transaction ID and payment method in the deal form, then click "Send Transaction & Deal Details to Cover". We'll verify it and notify you on WhatsApp.
How will I know what's happening with my deal?
All updates are sent directly to your WhatsApp. You'll get notifications when your payment is confirmed, when the seller ships, and when the funds are released.
How is the Cover fee calculated?
Cover charges a flat 5% fee on the total transaction amount (product price + delivery charges), with a minimum fee of ₨. 100. The deal form calculates this automatically as you type. You can also use the Fee Calculator above the form.
What if I receive a damaged or wrong item?
Do not confirm the deal. Instead, send an unboxing video and photos of the issue to Cover on WhatsApp immediately. Our team will review the evidence and mediate a fair dispute resolution.
Can I start a deal with any seller?
Yes! You can use Cover to protect transactions with any seller — whether you found them on Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, or anywhere else. Just enter their details in the deal form.
Where does my money go during the hold?
Your money goes into a dedicated Cover hold account, completely separate from our operating funds. It is never invested or used. You can request proof of the hold balance anytime.
Who pays the delivery charges?
Delivery charges are included in the total amount you pay to Cover upfront. If you return an item because it's faulty, the seller pays the return shipping. If you return it because you changed your mind, you pay the return shipping.
Is the process really manual?
Yes, during our private beta phase, all deals and verifications are handled manually by our team to ensure maximum security. We are actively building automated features for the future.

Return / Refund / Shipping Policy

Last updated: January 2026

Return Policy

You may initiate a return if the product received does not match the description agreed upon at the time of deal submission. Returns must be reported within 24 hours of receiving the product. You must provide an unboxing video as evidence. Cover does not accept returns for buyer's remorse, change of mind, or minor cosmetic differences clearly visible in pre-deal photos.

Refund Policy

  • Wrong item received: Full refund of the held amount (minus Cover's protection fee is non-refundable).
  • Damaged or defective item: Full refund of the held amount after verification of unboxing video evidence.
  • Seller fails to ship: Full refund including Cover's protection fee.
  • Buyer cancels after payment is held: Refund subject to Cover's review. If the seller has already incurred costs (e.g., shipping), a partial refund may apply.
  • Dispute resolved in seller's favor: Payment released to seller; no refund to buyer.

Refunds are processed within 3–5 business days after dispute resolution. Refunds are sent via the same payment method used for the deal.

Shipping Policy

Cover facilitates deal protection — shipping is handled between the buyer and seller. The following rules apply:

  • Delivery charges: The buyer is responsible for delivery charges unless otherwise agreed in the deal terms.
  • Courier selection: Cover recommends using tracked courier services (e.g., TCS, Leopards) for all shipments. Untracked shipments are at the parties' own risk.
  • Shipping timeline: The seller must ship within the timeframe specified in the deal. If no timeframe was set, the default is 3 business days from payment hold confirmation.
  • Return shipping (buyer changed mind): If the product is correct but the buyer wants to return it (e.g., different variant preference), the buyer bears the return shipping cost.
  • Return shipping (seller fault): If the product is damaged, defective, or wrong — the seller bears the return shipping cost.

Unboxing Video Requirement

All buyers must record an unboxing video that clearly shows the package condition, product, and any defects. This video is mandatory for any return or refund claim. Claims without an unboxing video will not be accepted.

Dispute Resolution

If a dispute arises, both parties must submit evidence (photos, videos, chat screenshots) via WhatsApp. Cover's team reviews all evidence and makes a fair decision. Both parties agree to abide by Cover's dispute resolution outcome as part of using the service.
